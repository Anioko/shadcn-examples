## Business Strategy Frameworks

### Core Strategy & Planning

#### **Porter's Five Forces**
Competitive analysis framework examining:
- Threat of new entrants
- Bargaining power of suppliers
- Bargaining power of buyers
- Threat of substitute products
- Rivalry among existing competitors

#### **Porter's Generic Strategies**
- Cost Leadership
- Differentiation
- Focus (Cost or Differentiation)

#### **SWOT Analysis**
- **S**trengths (internal)
- **W**eaknesses (internal)
- **O**pportunities (external)
- **T**hreats (external)

#### **PESTLE/PEST Analysis**
Environmental scanning framework:
- **P**olitical
- **E**conomic
- **S**ocial
- **T**echnological
- **L**egal
- **E**nvironmental

#### **BCG Growth-Share Matrix**
Portfolio management tool:
- Stars (High growth, high market share)
- Cash Cows (Low growth, high market share)
- Question Marks (High growth, low market share)
- Dogs (Low growth, low market share)

#### **GE-McKinsey Nine-Box Matrix**
Strategic business unit analysis based on:
- Industry attractiveness
- Business unit strength

#### **Ansoff Matrix**

- Market Penetration

- Diversification

#### **Blue Ocean Strategy**
Creating uncontested market space through value innovation

#### **Red Ocean Strategy**

#### **Strategic Group Analysis**

#### **Core Competence Framework** (Prahalad & Hamel)

## Framework Selection Guidelines

### Key Considerations

#### **1. Organizational Context**
- Size and complexity
- Industry and market dynamics
- Geographic scope
- Organizational maturity

#### **2. Strategic Objectives**
- Growth vs. optimization
- Innovation vs. efficiency
- Short-term vs. long-term focus
- Competitive positioning

#### **3. Resources and Capabilities**
- Available expertise
- Time constraints
- Budget limitations
- Technology infrastructure

#### **4. Cultural Fit**
- Change readiness
- Leadership style
- Communication preferences
- Risk tolerance

### Framework Integration Patterns

#### **Common Combinations**

**Startup Stack:**
- Business Model Canvas (strategy)
- Lean Startup (execution)
- OKRs (alignment)
- Pirate Metrics (measurement)

**Enterprise Stack:**
- TOGAF/ArchiMate (architecture)
- Balanced Scorecard (performance)
- SAFe (delivery)
- COBIT (governance)

**Digital Transformation Stack:**
- Digital Transformation Framework (vision)
- Design Thinking (innovation)
- Agile/DevOps (delivery)
- Data Strategy Framework (enablement)

**Product Company Stack:**
- Jobs-to-be-Done (discovery)
- Value Proposition Canvas (design)
- Lean/Agile (development)
- Growth Hacking (scaling)

### Implementation Best Practices

1. **Start Simple** - Begin with lightweight frameworks
2. **Adapt, Don't Adopt** - Customize to your context
3. **Integrate Gradually** - Layer frameworks over time
4. **Measure Effectiveness** - Track framework impact
5. **Iterate Continuously** - Refine based on feedback
6. **Build Capability** - Train teams properly
7. **Maintain Flexibility** - Avoid framework rigidity
8. **Document Decisions** - Capture rationale and evolution

## Conclusion

This comprehensive collection represents decades of strategic thinking and organizational learning. The most successful organizations:

- **Combine** multiple frameworks strategically
- **Adapt** frameworks to their unique context
- **Balance** structure with flexibility
- **Focus** on outcomes over process
- **Evolve** their approach continuously

The key is not to find the "perfect" framework, but to select and integrate frameworks that enable better decisions, clearer communication, and more effective execution aligned with your strategic objectives.
